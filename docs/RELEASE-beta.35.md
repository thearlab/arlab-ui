# ARLAB UI 2.0.0-beta.35

Department icons keep their solid identity color and metallic hover. Selection uses one thin 1px pink border, with no outer outline or double stroke. Keyboard focus remains independent and visible. Includes beta.34 neutral TextAction defaults and navigator WorkspaceGrid.

Pin github:thearlab/arlab-ui#v2.0.0-beta.35. Earlier tags remain immutable.

The shared foundation adds surface-inset-shadow and chrome-inset-shadow roles: a brighter inner highlight and soft bottom-edge shadow for containers, dialogs, buttons and icons. Fields keep their recessed finish with a slightly stronger inner edge; tags use a lighter version. Hover elevation increases subtly. Palette, gradients, dimensions and focus behavior remain unchanged.

The collapsed rail centers the canonical square ARLAB mark. The LogOut icon registry entry now uses Lucide’s clear door-and-arrow artwork, automatically updating AccountMenu and consumers of the shared icon.

A canonical transparent ARLAB-symbol favicon is available at @arlab/ui/favicon.svg. Light/dark pink follows the approved palette; no black tile, background or custom mark. Applications can copy it to their public assets during build.
